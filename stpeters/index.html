Rooted in History.
Moving in Purpose.

Founded in 1866 in the aftermath of the Civil War, St. Peter's United Methodist Church was born out of a community's unshakeable faith and determination to worship freely. For over 160 years, we have gathered, grown, and served the people of Austin.

Today, that same spirit of resilience and love drives everything we do — from Sunday morning worship to midweek Bible study, from feeding families to mentoring youth. We are a living legacy, and you are invited to be part of it.
