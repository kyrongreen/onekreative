Who We Are.

A historic, Spirit-filled congregation committed to faith, fellowship, and transforming the Austin community through the love of Christ.

Our Mission

Seeking, Engaging & Encouraging Through Christ

At St. Peter's United Methodist Church, our mission is rooted in the Great Commission — to seek the lost, engage our community with the gospel, and encourage one another in faith. We are a church that believes every person carries divine purpose.

We pursue this mission through meaningful worship, deep discipleship, and hands-on community service. From the altar to the streets of East Austin, we are committed to making the love of Christ visible and tangible.

  • Historical Richness — Honoring 160 years of living faith
  • Spirit-Filled Worship — Encountering God every Sunday
  • Community Service — Loving our neighbors as ourselves
Our Beliefs

Grounded in the Methodist Tradition

We believe in the Holy Trinity — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. We affirm the full authority of Scripture as the inspired Word of God and the ultimate guide for Christian living.

As a United Methodist congregation, we embrace a theology of grace — prevenient grace that prepares the heart, justifying grace that saves, and sanctifying grace that transforms. We believe in the power of the Holy Spirit to change lives and communities.

We hold that the sacraments of baptism and holy communion are means of grace through which God works in the lives of believers. We welcome all to the table who seek the Lord with sincere hearts.
